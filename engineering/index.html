MY ENGINEERING APPROACH

Understand the System. Experiment with the Technology. Build Something Useful.

My approach to engineering is based on understanding how systems work rather than simply learning how to operate them. I am interested in the relationship between hardware, software, networks, electronics, and the physical world.

As a Computer Engineering student, I am developing a foundation that combines theoretical knowledge with practical experimentation. I use available tools, simulations, software environments, and hands-on projects to strengthen my understanding.
