Shedrack Nengak Bulus

Computer Engineering Student | Aspiring Network & Systems Engineer | Technology Builder

I am Shedrack Nengak Bulus, a 400-level Computer Engineering student at the Federal University Wukari, Nigeria. I am driven by curiosity about how technology works, how complex systems interact and how engineering knowledge can be transformed into practical solutions.

My technical journey spans several interconnected disciplines, including computer networking, cybersecurity, electronics, embedded systems, software development and digital engineering. I enjoy exploring technologies, experimenting with ideas and developing the skills required to design, build and troubleshoot systems.

My software interests include C and C++ programming, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and game development using raylib. I also explore Arduino-based projects, circuit simulation, 3D modelling with Blender, Linux systems and computer infrastructure.

Beyond software and digital systems, I appreciate practical engineering: electrical installation, motorcycle wiring, bicycle repair and metal fabrication. These experiences strengthen my appreciation for engineering as both a theoretical discipline and a hands-on craft.

I am currently undertaking my SIWES training at Gradenet ICT, Jalingo, Taraba State, specialising in Networking Engineering. This experience is part of my ongoing development in network infrastructure, routing, switching and communication technologies.

My long-term ambition is to build technology-driven ventures, develop useful solutions and establish a platform for continuous engineering exploration, innovation and entrepreneurship.
